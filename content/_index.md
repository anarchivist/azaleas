This site exists to compile [translations]({{% ref "poems/translations/_index.md" %}}) and [transformations]({{% ref "poems/transformations/_index.md" %}}) of the poem <ruby><rb>[**진달래꽃**]({{% ref "진달래꽃" %}})</rb><rp>(</rp><rt>Jindallaekkot</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> (tr. "Azaleas") by <ruby><rb>Kim Sowol</rb><rp>(</rp><rt>김소월</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. It was prepared in service of the September 17, 2026 [Unpoetry at the Frye performance]({{% ref "performance.md" %}}) with María Dolores A. Matienzo, Tom Scully, Noelle K. Chun, Tori Winkler, and Eric M. Acosta. The performance is an ekphrastic response to the [Lotus L. Kang](https://lotuslkang.com) exhibition [*I hear the hollow boom of time*](https://fryemuseum.org/exhibitions/lotus-l-kang-i-hear-hollow-boom-time).

All translations whenever possible are sourced with a citation to their original source, or where the original source is not known, the source in which the translation was found.
