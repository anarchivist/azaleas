This site exists to compile translations of the poem [**진달래꽃**]({{% ref "진달래꽃.md" %}}) ("Jindallaekkot", tr. "Azaleas") by Kim Sowol (김소월). It was prepared  in service of the e September 17, 2026 [Unpoetry at the Frye](https://fryemuseum.org/calendar/event/unpoetry-frye-september-2026) performance with María Dolores A. Matienzo, Tom Scully, Noelle K. Chun, Tori Winkler, and Eric M. Acosta. The performance is an ekphrastic response to the [Lotus L. Kang](https://lotuslkang.com) exhibition [*I hear the hollow boom of time*](https://fryemuseum.org/exhibitions/lotus-l-kang-i-hear-hollow-boom-time). The performers filmed a version of their performance inside Kang's sculpture *Azaleas III*. Kang has written and spoken about the influence of the poem on her work, especially the *Azaleas* series. Accordingly, these translations were compiled from a handful of sources to inform a process of residual poetics used in the execution of the performance.

All translations whenever possible are sourced with a citation to their original source, or where the original source is not known, the source in which the translation was found.
