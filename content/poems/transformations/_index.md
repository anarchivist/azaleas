These transformations are derived from the "poetic residue" collected translations, which were treated as a corpus for practices like erasure and computational text manipulation. In addition to the translation selected for the [performance]({{% ref "poems/performance/index.md" %}}), these transformations were read to present new lenses on what already exists: a poem with many interpretations.
