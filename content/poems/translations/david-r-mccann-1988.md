---
title: Azaleas
translator: David R. McCann
source: |
    David R. McCann. "The Meanings and Significance of So Wŏls 'Azaleas'." _Journal of Korean Studies_ 6(1) (1988). <https://doi.org/10.1353/jks.1988.0004>.
---

Weary of seeing me, when you  
go away,  
without a word, gently,  
I shall let you   
go. 

From Mount Yak  
in Yŏngbyŏn,  
armfuls of azaleas  
I shall gather and scatter on your way.

As you go, step by step  
on the flowers placed before you  
tread lightly,  
softly as you go.
