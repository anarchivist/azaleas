---
title: |
    Azaleas
translator: Jaihiun J. Kim
source: |
    _Korean poetry today: 450 poems since the 1920's._ Jaihiun J. Kim, ed. and tr. Seoul: Hanshin, 1987.
---

If you go away  
Through with me  
I will quietly let you go.

I will gather azaleas in armfuls  
At Yaksan hill in Yongbyon  
To scatter them on your path.

Tread with a tread,  
Light and gentle,  
On the flowers as you go.  

If you go away  
Through with me  
Never will I weep though I perish.
