---
title: Azaleas
translator: Kim Jong-Gil
source: |
    Kim Ki-Mun, editor. _Korea Through her Arts_. Seoul: Yesul Chunchu, 1964.
---

When you leave,  
Weary of me,  
I'll gently let you go.

I will gather
Armfuls of azaleas  
From Yaksan to adorn your path.

Tread softly,  
Step by step,  
Upon the flowers as you go.

When you leave,  
Weary of me,  
I'll bite my lips to stop my tears.
