---
title: "Azaleas"
author: Kim So Wol
translator: Kim Woo Chul
source: |
    Kim Woo Chul. "The Native Place of Kim So Wol". _New Korea_ 30. Pyongyang: Foreign Languages Publishing House, 1958.
---

Should you leave me,  
Saying you're weary of me,  
No word would I speak.

Only with azaleas  
From Yaksan, Pyongbook  
{{< indent 10 >}} Nyongbyon,  
Would I carpet your way.

Tread on, my dearest—  
With lovely petals  
I will weave your way.  

If you grow weary of me,  
No tears would I shed  
Though I may die.
