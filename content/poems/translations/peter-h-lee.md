---
title: The Azalea
translator: Peter H. Lee
source: |
    _Anthology of Korean Poetry from the earliest era to the present._ Peter H. Lee, ed. New York: John Day, 1964.
---

You're sick and tired of me.  
When you go  
I'll bid you goodbye without saying a word.

I'll gather  
azaleas on Yak Mountain,  
the burning azaleas of Yöngbyon,  
and strew them in your path.


Tread gently, please,  
step by step, softly,  
on the flowers of dedication.

You're sick and tired of me.  
When you leave  
I'll not weep though I die.
