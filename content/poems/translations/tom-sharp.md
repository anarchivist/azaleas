---
title: "Azalea petals"
translator: Tom Sharp
source: |
    Sharp, Tom. "Azalea petals." _Fifty Loose Translations_. <https://sharpgiving.com/Sharp/LooseTranslations/poems/korean1-both.html>
---

Let azaleas grow abundantly  
around where I am buried  

so when you come to stomp on my grave  
your feet will be covered with petals

and you will want to come back often  
even though you hate me passionately.
