---
title: Azaleas
translator: Wayne De Fremery
source: |
    Peter Wayne De Fremery. _How Poetry Mattered in 1920s Korea._ 2011. Harvard University, PhD dissertation. _ProQuest_, <https://www.proquest.com/docview/879405777?accountid=14496>.
---

When you leave,  
sickened by the sight of me,  
I will send you away without a word.  

I will scatter an armful of  
azaleas from Yaksan in Yŏngbyŏn  
over the path you leave by.

Go, and let each footfall fall  
gently, tenderly  
on the flowers I have left for you.  

When you leave,  
sickened by the sight of me,  
even if I die, no tears will fall.
