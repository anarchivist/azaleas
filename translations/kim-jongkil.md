---
title: Azaleas
translator: Jongkil Kim
source: |
    Chung Han-mo. _Korean Poetry: An anthology with Critical Essays._ Seoul: The Korean Culture & Arts Foundation, 1984.
---

When you leave,  
Grown weary of me  
I will gently let you go.

An armful of azaleas  
From Yaksan, Yongbyon,  
I will gather to adorn your path.

Tread softly,  
Step by step,  
Upon the flowers as you go.

When you leave,  
Grown weary of me,  
I will bite my lips to stop my tears.
