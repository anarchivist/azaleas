---
title: Untitled attitudinal translation
translator: Myong Kyo Jeong
---

> I will hold back the tears that I should be shedding, she says; that amount of sadness will not be reduced, but will build up in my heart, leading those azalea petals to fester as your “possession” of happiness. I shall not shed a tear, so tread upon them as [you] go. No matter what. No matter what, I shall not shed a tear.
